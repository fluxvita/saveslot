# Maintainers

The maintainers of saveslot review and merge pull requests, triage issues and discussions, and publish
releases. What they decide and how someone becomes one is in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).

The current maintainers are the members of the GitHub team
[`@fluxvita/saveslot-maintainers`](https://github.com/orgs/fluxvita/teams/saveslot-maintainers): the team
is the list, kept on GitHub rather than in this file. Mention the team in an issue or discussion to
reach them; report security issues as [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) says, and
conduct issues to [opensource@fluxvita.com](mailto:opensource@fluxvita.com).
